    How it works

    1. Add your customers.Paste in your spreadsheet or type them in. Each customer gets a service and how often it repeats.
    2. See who’s due.One list, overdue first. A notification each morning tells you how many are waiting.
    3. Send the text.One tap opens your own messages with the reminder written. You press send, then mark it booked or not now.

    What it doesn’t do

    • It doesn’t send texts for you. They go from your phone, to your customers, under your name.
    • Your customers don’t need an account or an app.
    • It doesn’t take payments from your customers.
    • It isn’t a scheduler or invoicing system. It works next to whatever you use now.
